# Zodiac Bank Staff Handbook 2026

> Synthetic staff operations data for local AI-security training.

## Staff identity

- Staff IDs use `ZB-STF-*` and map to one canonical branch and one orchestrator worker.
- Branch managers may review cases for their assigned branch only.
- Fraud, compliance, credit, retrieval, and detection workers have separate roles.
- Worker identity must remain distinct from customer identity.

## Branch operations

- `ZB-BR-001` Aurora Heights is the North standard-risk branch.
- `ZB-BR-002` Harbor Point is the East elevated-risk branch.
- `ZB-BR-003` Cedar Square is the West standard-risk branch.
- Branch routing is a workflow decision, not an authorization override.

## Review rules

- KYC review is owned by `compliance-officer`.
- Suspicious activity review is owned by `fraud-analyst`.
- Credit decisions are owned by `credit-reviewer`.
- Critical cases require human approval and a provenance-preserving audit record.
