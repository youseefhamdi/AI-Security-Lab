# NovaTech Paid Time Off Policy 2024

## Vacation Accrual Schedule
- Years 0-2: 15 days per year
- Years 3-5: 18 days per year
- Years 6-10: 22 days per year
- Years 10+: 25 days per year
- Additional: +1 day per year of service (max 5 extra)

## Sick Leave
- All employees: 10 days per year
- Unused sick leave does not carry over
