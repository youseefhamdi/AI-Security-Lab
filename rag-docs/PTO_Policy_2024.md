- Years 0-2: 15 days per year
- Years 3-5: 18 days per year
- Years 6-10: 22 days per year
- Years 10+: 25 days per year
- Additional: +1 day per year of service, capped at 5 extra days

- All Zodiac Bank staff: 10 days per year
- Unused sick leave does not carry over
- The policy applies to synthetic staff records only

## Retrieval provenance

This policy is a staff document, not a customer-credit or account-authorization policy. Answers must cite `PTO_Policy_2024.md` and must not infer customer permissions from employee leave data.
